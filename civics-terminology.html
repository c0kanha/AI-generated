Civics Test Terminology

Plain-English (and Telugu) meanings for the words that get mixed up in the USCIS 2025 civics test. Use the search box to jump to a term.

The Legislative Branch (Congress)

Congress

కాంగ్రెస్ — చట్టాలు చేసే శాసన శాఖ

The legislative branch of the federal government — the part that makes laws.

Congress is made up of two parts together: the Senate and the House of Representatives. So "Congress" = Senate + House. It has 100 + 435 = 535 voting members.

Senate

సెనేట్ — కాంగ్రెస్ ఎగువ సభ

The upper house of Congress. It has 100 Senators — 2 from every state, no matter how big or small the state is.

A Senator serves a 6-year term.

House of Representatives

హౌస్ ఆఫ్ రిప్రజెంటేటివ్స్ — కాంగ్రెస్ దిగువ సభ

The lower house of Congress. It has 435 voting members (Representatives). The number from each state depends on the state's population — bigger states get more.

A Representative serves a 2-year term. Often just called "the House."

Senator

సెనేటర్ — సెనేట్ సభ్యుడు

A member of the Senate. Each state has 2 U.S. Senators who represent all people of the state.

Representative (Congressman / Congresswoman)

రిప్రజెంటేటివ్ — హౌస్ సభ్యుడు

A member of the House of Representatives. Each one represents one district of a state (not the whole state).

Speaker of the House

స్పీకర్ — హౌస్ నాయకుడు

The leader of the House of Representatives. Chosen by the members of the House.

The Executive Branch

President

అధ్యక్షుడు — దేశాధినేత

The head of the executive branch. The President signs bills into law, is Commander in Chief of the military, and is in charge of the federal government. Term: 4 years.

Vice President

ఉపాధ్యక్షుడు

Becomes President if the President can no longer serve. Also serves as President of the Senate.

Cabinet

కేబినెట్ — అధ్యక్షుని సలహా బృందం

A group of advisors to the President (the Secretaries — e.g., Secretary of State, Secretary of Defense). They advise the President.

The Judicial Branch

Supreme Court

సుప్రీం కోర్టు — అత్యున్నత న్యాయస్థానం

The highest court in the United States. It reviews laws and decides if they follow the Constitution. It has 9 justices.

Chief Justice

ప్రధాన న్యాయమూర్తి

The head of the Supreme Court (and the head of the judicial branch).

Other Key Roles & Words

U.S. Diplomat / Ambassador

దౌత్యవేత్త / రాయబారి

An official who represents the United States in other countries and handles relations with foreign governments. (Benjamin Franklin is famous as the "first U.S. diplomat.")

Governor

గవర్నర్ — రాష్ట్ర అధినేత

The head of a state's government (state level, not federal). Compare: the President leads the whole country; a Governor leads one state.

Civic duty / Civic responsibility

పౌర బాధ్యత

Things a citizen is expected to do for the country and community — for example serving on a jury and voting in a federal election, paying taxes, and obeying the law.

"Civic" = relating to citizens and the duties of being a citizen.

Three branches of government

ప్రభుత్వ మూడు శాఖలు

The government is split into three parts so no one part has too much power: Legislative (Congress — makes laws), Executive (President — carries out laws), Judicial (courts — explains laws).

Checks and balances / Separation of powers

అధికార సమతుల్యత

The system where each branch can limit the power of the other branches, so no single branch becomes too strong.

Rule of law

చట్ట పాలన

The idea that everyone must follow the law — including leaders, the government, and ordinary people. No one is above the law.

The Constitution

రాజ్యాంగం — దేశ అత్యున్నత చట్టం

The supreme law of the land. It sets up the government and protects the basic rights of the people.

Amendment

సవరణ — రాజ్యాంగానికి మార్పు

A change or addition to the Constitution. The first 10 amendments are the Bill of Rights. There are 27 amendments in all.

Bill of Rights

హక్కుల పట్టిక

The first 10 amendments to the Constitution, listing basic freedoms and rights of the people.

Bill / Veto

బిల్లు / వీటో

A bill is a proposed law. Congress writes bills; the President signs them to make them law. To veto means the President rejects a bill.
